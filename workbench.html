What it does

  • Imports general ledger, profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, payroll and timesheet files, including PDFs
  • Classifies expenditure across 19 categories and applies R&D percentages with a cascading fallback resolver
  • Applies Division 355 rules, including feedstock adjustments, associate payment timing, and s 355-225 exclusions
  • Calculates the refundable and non-refundable offset positions against aggregated turnover
  • Produces a working-paper schedule with the reasoning recorded for review

Requirements

  • macOS 12 Monterey or later
  • Around 2 GB free disk space after installation
  • Internet connection for AI-assisted classification

Your data

Financial data is held in a local database on your own machine. Files you import are not uploaded anywhere. AI-assisted classification sends only the extracted account descriptions needed for the task, and R&D Tax Incentive processing is pinned to Australian regions with no offshore fallback.
